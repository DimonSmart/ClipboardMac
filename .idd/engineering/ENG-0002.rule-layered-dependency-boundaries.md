# ENG-0002.rule-layered-dependency-boundaries

## Rule

Organize dependencies as App/UI to application services to domain/contracts to
infrastructure, and keep AppKit, SQLite, and concrete hotkey libraries out of
domain storage and sorting logic.

## Applicability

Always

## Rationale

The boundary preserves testability and lets product behavior evolve without
binding domain decisions to system frameworks or a specific hotkey provider.

## Guidance

- Define contracts at the domain or application-service boundary for persistence,
  pasteboard access, and event posting.
- Keep UI and pasteboard operations in their required actor or thread context.
- Serialize database work and pass value data back to the interface explicitly.
- Do not suppress failures through broad ignored-error handling.

## Verification

Dependency review confirms that domain history behavior can be tested with
adapters and does not require AppKit, SQLite, or a concrete hotkey library.
