# IDD-0004.spec-history-panel-and-commands

## Intent

The history panel offers a quick, keyboard-accessible way to find and act on
saved clipboard text without turning the utility into a conventional app window.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines menu-bar lifecycle and supported scope.
- IDD-0003 defines the displayed history and search results.
- IDD-0005 defines the outcome of Paste and Copy Only actions.

## Behavior

- A left click on the menu-bar icon opens history. A right click opens the
  existing menu-bar command menu. Users can also open history with a configurable
  global Open History shortcut. The shortcut persists across launches; users
  choose it during first run or in Settings. Until it exists, the menu-bar
  command remains available.
- Unsafe or unavailable shortcuts, including ordinary paste, are rejected or
  explained. Changing the shortcut takes effect without restart. The product
  does not require Input Monitoring or globally observe all keyboard input.
- The same Open History shortcut closes an already open panel without inserting
  text; Escape and clicking outside do the same.
- The history panel provides standard close and minimize controls and supports
  native full-screen mode. Closing the window dismisses the panel without
  inserting text; reopening history restores the panel in a usable state.
- On each open, the search field is focused and empty. The first available entry
  is selected while ordinary typing continues into search. Arrow keys change
  selection; Enter or a single row click requests Paste; Command-Enter and the
  contextual Copy Only command request Copy Only. The panel title and header
  remain unobstructed by the window title bar. When the pointer is over an
  entry, that entry is visibly highlighted so the pending row-click target is
  clear; the highlight follows the pointer during scrolling and clears when no
  entry is under it. The list is sorted when the panel opens and keeps that
  order until it closes: changes to copy or pin times do not move existing
  entries, and newly captured entries are appended. Search filters the current
  list without changing its order. Double interaction must not execute two
  actions.
- The panel presents Search with a nearby Clear control that removes every
  unpinned history entry while preserving all pinned entries, and one compact
  ordered list of entries, with pinned
  entries first, previews that use the available row space to show copied text,
  pin controls, and contextual Paste, Copy Only, and Delete actions. Source and
  copy-time metadata are retained but are not shown in list rows. Preview text
  wraps within the available row width and collapses runs of whitespace, including
  line breaks, to a single space while
  preserving the saved text. Each entry has a clearly visible thin, slightly
  rounded outline. Its tight row spacing keeps more history visible without
  removing the full-content view on demand or providing an editor.
- The panel appears on the active display near its center, remains within the
  visible frame, and accounts for menu bar, Dock, multiple displays, and spaces.
  Its background is opaque and does not show content behind the panel. Its
  bounded, scrollable layout does not expand with very long text.
- The interface supports light and dark appearance, VoiceOver labels, and
  keyboard operation. It safely displays long and multiline Unicode text.
- Settings expose General controls for shortcut, launch at login, capture pause,
  and history limit; Privacy controls for clipboard and Accessibility state plus
  exclusions; and Data controls for clearing and storage information. First run
  explains operation, shortcut selection, and permissions without blocking
  Settings or repeatedly prompting after dismissal.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- The panel must be able to receive keyboard input while behaving as a transient
  floating surface.

## Non-goals

- The MVP does not require manual pin ordering or a separate entry editor.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can search, navigate, pin, delete, paste, or copy an entry entirely by
  keyboard.
- A user can clear all unpinned entries from the history panel without removing
  pinned entries.
- Pinning or recopying an entry while the panel is open does not move it; closing
  and reopening applies the current pin and copy-time ordering.
- A user can identify the entry a row click will paste by moving the pointer
  over the history list, and scrolling does not leave a stale hover highlight.
- The panel title and header remain fully visible below the window title bar.
- A user can close or minimize the panel using standard window controls and
  enter or leave full-screen mode using the standard full-screen control.
- Each clipboard entry is surrounded by a thin outline with slightly rounded
  corners. List rows wrap copied text within the available width, omit source
  and copy-time metadata, and collapse repeated whitespace and line breaks in
  the preview.
- A left click on the menu-bar icon opens history, and a right click opens the
  menu-bar command menu.
- The panel stays visible and operable on the active display and closes without
  action when dismissed. The panel background is opaque while it is open.

## Verification

- Verify shortcut registration and replacement, keyboard navigation, selected
  entry behavior, pointer-hover feedback for pasteable entries, accessibility
  labels, unobstructed panel title and header, thin rounded outlines around
  entries, hover feedback during scrolling, an opaque panel background, light
  and dark appearance, and
  multi-display placement on a real supported system.
- Verify that left and right clicks on the menu-bar icon open their respective
  history panel and command menu.
- Verify that clearing recent history from the panel removes all unpinned
  entries and preserves pinned entries.
- Verify close and minimize controls dismiss the panel without an entry action,
  and verify native full-screen entry and exit preserve panel usability.
