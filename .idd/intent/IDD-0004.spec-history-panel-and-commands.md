# IDD-0004.spec-history-panel-and-commands

## Intent

The history panel offers a quick, keyboard-accessible way to find and act on
saved clipboard text without turning the utility into a conventional app window.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines menu-bar lifecycle and supported scope.
- IDD-0003 defines the displayed history and search results.
- IDD-0005 defines the outcome of Paste and Copy Only actions.

## Behavior

- A left click on the menu-bar icon opens history. A right click opens the
  existing menu-bar command menu. Users can also open history with a configurable
  global Open History shortcut. The shortcut persists across launches; users
  choose it during first run or in Settings. Until it exists, the menu-bar
  command remains available.
- Unsafe or unavailable shortcuts, including ordinary paste, are rejected or
  explained. Changing the shortcut takes effect without restart. The product
  does not require Input Monitoring or globally observe all keyboard input.
- The same Open History shortcut closes an already open panel without inserting
  text; Escape and clicking outside do the same.
- On each open, the search field is focused and empty. The first available entry
  is selected while ordinary typing continues into search. Arrow keys change
  selection; Enter or a single row click requests Paste; Command-Enter and the
  contextual Copy Only command request Copy Only. Double interaction must not
  execute two actions.
- The panel presents Search, Pinned, Recent, concise one- or two-line previews,
  copy-time context, pin controls, and contextual Paste, Copy Only, and Delete
  actions. Full content is available on demand without providing an editor.
- The panel appears on the active display near its center, remains within the
  visible frame, and accounts for menu bar, Dock, multiple displays, and spaces.
  Its bounded, scrollable layout does not expand with very long text.
- The interface supports light and dark appearance, VoiceOver labels, and
  keyboard operation. It safely displays long and multiline Unicode text.
- Settings expose General controls for shortcut, launch at login, capture pause,
  and history limit; Privacy controls for clipboard and Accessibility state plus
  exclusions; and Data controls for clearing and storage information. First run
  explains operation, shortcut selection, and permissions without blocking
  Settings or repeatedly prompting after dismissal.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- The panel must be able to receive keyboard input while behaving as a transient
  floating surface.

## Non-goals

- The MVP does not require a resizable document window, manual pin ordering, or
  a separate entry editor.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can search, navigate, pin, delete, paste, or copy an entry entirely by
  keyboard.
- A left click on the menu-bar icon opens history, and a right click opens the
  menu-bar command menu.
- The panel stays visible and operable on the active display and closes without
  action when dismissed.

## Verification

- Verify shortcut registration and replacement, keyboard navigation, selected
  entry behavior, accessibility labels, light and dark appearance, and
  multi-display placement on a real supported system.
- Verify that left and right clicks on the menu-bar icon open their respective
  history panel and command menu.
