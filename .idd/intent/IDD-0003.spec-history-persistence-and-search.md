# IDD-0003.spec-history-persistence-and-search

## Intent

Clipboard History keeps a durable, searchable collection of unique copied text
and protects pinned entries from ordinary retention and clearing.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines local persistence and lifecycle.
- IDD-0002 defines what content can enter history.
- IDD-0004 defines panel presentation and history commands.

## Behavior

- Recent history contains at most 500 unpinned unique texts by default. Users
  may set the limit from 50 through 2000. Pinned entries are outside this
  limit and are never automatically evicted.
- Recopying exactly the same UTF-8 text updates its last-copied time and known
  source, moves it among Recent, and does not create a duplicate. Matching is
  exact and case-sensitive; a hash may narrow candidates only when the full text
  is also compared.
- Recopying a pinned entry updates its copied metadata without unpinning it or
  creating a Recent duplicate.
- After an add, recopy, or unpin, the oldest unpinned entries exceeding the
  configured limit are removed deterministically. Mutations that change history
  or its indexes are atomic.
- Users can pin or unpin any entry. Pinned entries appear before Recent, ordered
  by pin time with newest first. Unpinning returns an entry to Recent according
  to last-copied time and immediately applies retention.
- Users can delete any individual entry. Clear Recent removes only unpinned
  entries. Clear All removes every entry only after explicit confirmation.
- Saved history and pins remain visible after restart even when fresh capture is
  unavailable, with an appropriate status shown.
- Search considers every entry, including pins, by case-insensitive Unicode-aware
  substring matching for Russian, English, and accented text. Search results
  remain separated into Pinned and Recent; no results shows “No matches”.
- Search and normal panel updates remain responsive with 500 Recent entries and
  a reasonable number of pins. Long text is shortened only for display, never
  in its stored value.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

- History changes and bulk clearing must not leave partial records or orphaned
  stored content. Storage maintenance must prevent unbounded growth from normal
  fill-and-clear cycles without blocking the interface.

## Non-goals

- The MVP provides neither manual pin ordering nor editing of stored text.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A repeated copy yields one entry, while text differing only by a trailing
  space yields another.
- Pins survive history overflow, Clear Recent, crashes after committed changes,
  and restart.
- Clear All requires confirmation and removes pins as well as Recent entries.

## Verification

- Verify deduplication, pinning, retention, clear operations, restart recovery,
  Unicode search, and data-integrity behavior with automated coverage.
- Verify performance and storage behavior while filling, clearing, and reopening
  history near its configured limit.
